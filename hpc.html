About CDAC

The Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) is the premier R&D organization of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India. CDAC is the driving force behind India’s supercomputing mission, national digital infrastructure, and cutting-edge technologies in AI, cybersecurity, and IoT.

CDAC HPC Supercomputers

PARAM Siddhi-AI

India’s fastest supercomputer with 5.267 Petaflops performance. Ranked in the Top 100 supercomputers globally.

PARAM Brahma

High-performance computing system at IISER Pune, enabling research in climate, chemistry, and advanced physics.

PARAM Shavak

A compact "Supercomputer-in-a-Box" designed for academic institutions and small enterprises.

Major Achievements

  • First Indian supercomputer PARAM 8000 (1991).
  • PARAM Siddhi ranked 62nd in the world (TOP500 list 2020).
  • Key contributor to the National Supercomputing Mission (NSM).
  • Deployment of supercomputers across IITs, IISERs, and National labs.
